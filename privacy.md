# Privacy Policy – SURE finances

SURE finances is a privately hosted personal finance application operated by Momme Vonthin.

The application is used exclusively for personal financial management and retrieves account balances and transaction history from the operator's own bank accounts.

Bank data is retrieved through Enable Banking Oy after authorization by the account holder. The retrieved information is transferred to and stored on the operator's privately managed server.

The data is used solely for personal budgeting, financial overview and expense analysis. It is not sold, shared publicly or used for commercial purposes.
